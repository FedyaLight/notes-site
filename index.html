Notes

A personal native app for macOS and Android: notes, a film diary, a calendar and personal finances.

Your records are stored on your device. Nearby devices can sync over the local network. Optional Google Drive sync relays encrypted records between your own devices when they are apart.

Drive access is limited to the hidden app data folder. Notes cannot browse your other Drive files. Use the same Google account and sync code on both devices.

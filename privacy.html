Notes privacy policy

Effective October 24, 2026.

Notes stores your notes, images, movie records, calendar and finance records locally on your devices. It has no developer-operated sync server, analytics or advertising.

Optional Google Drive sync

When you connect Google Drive, Notes requests only the drive.appdata scope. It reads and writes encrypted snapshots and note images in its own hidden application data folder to sync your personal records between your devices. It does not access your ordinary Drive files, Gmail, contacts or other Google services.

The sync code you enter derives the encryption key. Use a strong code and keep it safe: the application cannot recover encrypted data without it. A copy remains on each synced device. Google stores the encrypted relay files under your account and its own service terms.

On macOS, OAuth refresh credentials are stored in the login Keychain. On Android, Google Play services manages the authorization tokens; Notes stores the selected account name in private app storage. Tokens are used only to access the application data folder. They are not sent to the developer.

Disconnecting stops access from that device and removes its stored authorization state. The Drive copy remains for recovery and other devices. You can revoke Notes access in your Google Account third-party access settings, and delete the hidden app data in Google Drive settings under Manage apps. Local data is controlled separately on each device.

Deletion markers are retained to prevent an offline device from restoring deleted records. Previously uploaded encrypted image blobs remain in the Drive application data until you delete that app data.

Movie catalog and artwork

Catalog searches and artwork requests contact Wikipedia, Wikidata and artwork hosts. They receive the request and normal network information, including the IP address. Your private ratings and diaries are not uploaded to catalog services.

Google API data

Notes uses Google API data only for the user-requested sync feature, in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. It does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, share it with data brokers, or use it to train AI models.

For questions, use the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
